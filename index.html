Imports & Exports Clearing Services

Imports:

  • Please Attach the Following For Your Imports:
  • Commercial Invoice;
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill;
  • Certificate of Origin;
  • Packing List;
  • Import Declaration Form;
  • Proof of Payment (e.g., receipt or bank slip);
  • Relevant Licenses or Permits (if applicable);
  • Insurance Certificate (if insured).

    • The faster you send these documents the faster we can speed up the clearing process.

    Exports:

  • Please Attach the Following For Your Exports
    • :
  • Obtain an export permit or license (if required);
  • Comply with export regulations and laws;
  • Declare the goods being exported;
  • Pay any applicable export duties or taxes;
  • Obtain a customs clearance document (e.g., export declaration.

    • The faster you send these documents the faster we can speed up the clearing process.

    Shipment Handling & Equipment Services

    Forklifts:

    Forklifts are the backbone of efficient cargo handling at any company. With their versatility and maneuverability, our forklifts enable swift and safe loading/unloading of goods, reducing labor costs and increasing productivity.

    Pallet Jacks:

    Pallet jacks are ideal for navigating tight spaces and handling heavy pallets with ease. Our pallet jacks streamline warehouse operations, minimize congestion, and enhance efficiency.

    Container Handlers:

    Container handlers are designed to provide precise control and flexibility in managing containers. We utilize container handlers to ensure safe and efficient cargo handling operations, minimizing the risk of damage or accidents.

    Reach Stackers:

    Reach stackers offer unparalleled flexibility and precision in container handling. Reach stackers enable efficient stacking and retrieval of containers, maximizing storage capacity and minimizing dwell times.

    Gantry Cranes:

    Gantry cranes provide heavy-lifting capabilities for oversized cargo. At AFS, our gantry cranes ensure safe and precise handling of project cargo, reducing the risk of damage or accidents.

    Straddle Carriers:

    Straddle carriers are the backbone of AFS’s container handling operations. With their exceptional maneuverability and lifting capacity, these straddle carriers enable AFS to efficiently move and stack containers, reducing dwell times and increasing productivity.

    Delivery, Dispatch & Cartage Services

    Tractor trailers are the workhorses of our delivery fleet, enabling efficient transportation of large volumes of cargo. We ensure timely and cost-effective movement of goods, reducing transit times and increasing your satisfaction.

    Pickup vans offer agile and flexible delivery solutions for smaller shipments. They enable rapid collection and delivery of goods, catering to your needs of fast-paced logistics requirements.

    Truck vans provide a versatile and reliable solution for cargo transportation. We ensure secure and efficient movement of goods, adapting to the diverse needs of you, our clients and guaranteeing on-time delivery.

    Dispatch riders offer a speedy and efficient solution for urgent deliveries. Our dispatch riders navigate through traffic with ease, adding a layer of flexibility for logistics needs.
